Who We Are

Next Stage Talent Is a Casting and Representation Agency

Next Stage Talent is a talent representation and casting agency based in London. We submit our talent for real paid roles in film, television, theatre, commercials, and advertising. We use Spotlight and direct industry relationships to put the right talent in front of the right casting directors.

We do not run workshops. We do not sell courses. We do not charge actors or models to join. We earn 20% commission when we secure paid work for the talent we represent — that is the only money that ever changes hands.

Free for talent to join: When you apply to Next Stage Talent, there is no fee to pay. You get a dedicated agent, a Castly profile, a Z-card, and access to our fortnightly job lists. We only earn when you earn.

Common Questions

About Next Stage Talent

What type of agency is Next Stage Talent?

Next Stage Talent is a casting and talent representation agency. We represent actors, models, extras, presenters, and commercial talent. We submit talent for paid roles in film, TV, theatre, commercials, and advertising. We are not a workshop provider and we do not run training courses.

How does Next Stage Talent make money?

We take a standard 20% commission only when we successfully secure paid work for talent we represent. We do not charge any fees upfront and we have no ongoing subscription or retainer costs. No work secured means no payment to us.

Is Next Stage Talent on Spotlight?

Yes. Next Stage Talent is a registered agency on Spotlight, the UK's leading casting platform used by casting directors across film, TV, theatre, and commercials. Our agents use Spotlight to submit talent for industry opportunities.

Can I be with other agencies while represented by Next Stage Talent?

Yes. Next Stage Talent operates on an open contract. You are completely free to be registered with other agencies at the same time. There is no exclusivity requirement and no lock-in period.
