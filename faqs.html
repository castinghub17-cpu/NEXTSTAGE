Next Stage Talent: Frequently Asked Questions

Every question answered on fees, portfolio, castings, commission and more.

Whether you've just found us through a search for "Next Stage Talent FAQ", or you're about to apply and want to understand how we work. This page answers every question we're regularly asked. Fully transparent, no waffle.

Joining Next Stage Talent
How do I join Next Stage Talent? +
Fill in our online application form or email us directly. We review all applications and reply within 5 business days. There is no registration fee — applying is completely free.
Does Next Stage Talent charge a registration fee? +
No. Next Stage Talent does not charge any upfront registration or joining fee. We operate on a commission-only model, taking 20% only when we secure paid work for you. No legitimate agency should ever charge you to register.
What types of talent does Next Stage Talent represent? +
We represent models, actors, presenters, dancers, extras, and commercial talent of all ages, sizes, and backgrounds — across film, television, commercials, theatre, fashion, and advertising.
Can I submit my portfolio online? +
Yes — use our contact form with a link to your portfolio, or attach photos directly to your application. You can apply at nextstagetalent.co.uk/apply.
What areas does Next Stage Talent cover? +
We are based in London but work nationally across the UK and internationally. Remote castings are increasingly common, so your location is rarely a barrier to being represented by us.
Portfolio & Photography
Is a portfolio necessary to join Next Stage Talent? +
Professional images are not required to apply, but a high-quality portfolio is essential for casting submissions. Think of it as your CV — casting directors make quick decisions based on what they see. Many trained actors and models already have suitable photos and come to us without needing new ones. If your images are not yet industry-standard, we can help arrange access to reputable studios.
Do I need to pay for my portfolio? +
Yes, if you need new photos, you invest in your own portfolio. This payment goes directly to an independent photography studio — not to Next Stage Talent. We arrange access to reputable studios at discounted rates exclusively for our talent, but we do not profit from this arrangement. Some top agencies fund portfolios themselves, but they recoup this from your first earnings — we prefer to be upfront about costs from the start.
What exactly does the portfolio fee cover? Is it Next Stage Talent's fee? +
The portfolio fee is not our fee. It is paid directly to a third-party studio that we arrange on your behalf. We do not handle photography in-house and we do not keep any portion of this payment. Many trained actors from drama schools already have professional headshots and bring them to us directly — the studio arrangement is simply there to help those who need it reach the required standard.
Do professional actors or Spotlight members still need a portfolio? +
No. If you are already a professional actor or a Spotlight member, your profile is considered complete and industry-ready. You do not need to invest in a new portfolio — we can represent you straight away. We simply take our standard 20% commission on any work we secure for you. Nothing else.
Does Next Stage Talent provide photography services? +
Next Stage Talent does not operate an in-house photography service. However, once you are selected to join our roster, we are happy to help if you need photography. We collaborate with reputable third-party studios that offer discounted rates exclusively for our talent, ensuring you can secure high-quality, industry-standard images at a competitive price.
What is the refund and cancellation policy for the portfolio shoot? +
As the shoot is arranged through an independent studio — who must book their team, photographer, and resources well in advance, often at a discounted rate — the studio operates a no-refund policy once a date is confirmed. This is standard practice across professional photography studios. We strongly encourage you to be confident in your decision before confirming a shoot date, and to ask us any questions beforehand.
Fees & Commission
What commission does Next Stage Talent take? +
We take a standard 20% commission on work we successfully secure for you. This is the only payment Next Stage Talent ever receives. If we don't secure work, we don't earn.
Are there any ongoing or additional fees? +
None whatsoever. We do not charge any retainer fees, subscription costs, annual charges, or ongoing fees of any kind. All of our services (submissions, representation, casting outreach) are completely free to you. The only time money changes hands is when we secure paid work for you, at which point we take our standard 20% commission. That's it.
What services does Next Stage Talent provide for free? +
Quite a lot. Here is exactly what every Next Stage Talent member receives free of charge as part of their representation:
  • A dedicated agent assigned to you personally
  • Your profile created and managed on Castly (our industry partner platform)
  • A Z-card included at no cost
  • Lifetime basic membership on Castly (normally £4.99 per month, worth £59.88 per year), free for all Next Stage Talent members for life
  • Self-tape guidance and example videos to help you meet the standard casting directors expect

There are no hidden costs, no subscriptions, and no ongoing charges. The only payment Next Stage Talent ever receives is a 20% commission when we secure paid work for you.

How much do I get paid for jobs through Next Stage Talent? +
Pay rates vary based on the specific project, your role, and level of experience. Rates are set by the client (production company or brand), not by us, and we negotiate on your behalf. Commercial and TV work tends to pay more than extras work. We will always inform you of the rate before you confirm any booking.
Is 20% commission standard for talent agencies? +
Yes. A 20% commission rate is entirely standard across UK talent and casting agencies. Some charge more; we keep ours at the industry benchmark.
Castings & How It Works
How does Next Stage Talent find casting jobs for talent? +
We work directly with casting directors, production companies, and brands. Our agents personally send talent details to casting teams based on the brief, your profile, and your availability. We do not rely on automated submissions through third-party platforms. In addition to putting you forward for roles, we provide personalised guidance to help you prepare for auditions.
How are jobs allocated to talent? +
We match talent to castings based on the client's brief, your profile, and your availability. We don't submit everyone for every role — we make targeted, relevant submissions, which is more effective and more professional than a blanket approach.
Are jobs guaranteed through Next Stage Talent? +
No, and any agency claiming otherwise should raise a flag for you. Casting decisions are made by casting directors and production companies based on their specific requirements at any given time — that part is outside anyone's control. Our honest advice: if you're looking for a quick guaranteed return, this industry is unlikely to be the right fit. Success in talent and acting requires genuine passion, hard work, and patience.
What happens after I apply to Next Stage Talent? +
We review your application and aim to respond within 5 business days. Shortlisted candidates are invited to an initial interview via Zoom with one of our talent agents — a chance for us to assess your personality, confidence, and skillset, and for you to learn more about us. From there, we'll discuss next steps including portfolio assessment and representation.
Can I be represented by Next Stage Talent and another agency at the same time? +
Yes, absolutely. Next Stage Talent operates on an open contract, meaning you are completely free to be registered with other agencies simultaneously. This is very common in the industry and we actively encourage talent to maximise their opportunities.
How long does representation with Next Stage Talent last? +
We operate on an open contract with no fixed time frame. You remain with us for as long as the relationship is working for both parties. There is no lock-in period and no cancellation fee.
Is Next Stage Talent on Spotlight? +
Yes. Our agents are registered on Spotlight, one of the UK's most widely used and respected casting platforms for acting, theatre, and commercial work. This gives our talent access to exclusive casting calls from a wide range of production companies and casting directors.
Our Practices Explained
Why don't you display client CVs publicly? +
Most of our talent are covered under NDA agreements. This means we are contractually permitted to share their names publicly, but not their full CVs or personal details. Sharing a client's full CV without their consent would breach their privacy. Casting directors who enquire about a specific person receive full details directly through us.
Did Next Stage Talent previously list ages publicly for under-18 talent? +
Yes, we did previously display ages as a practical way to help casting teams identify suitable talent. Following feedback, we reviewed this practice and have since removed ages from our public listings. We took that feedback seriously and updated our approach accordingly.
Why were Spotlight job listings showing on Next Stage Talent pages? +
Any Spotlight job listings that appeared on our pages were part of a test page that was never intended to go live. It was a technical error and those pages have since been removed. We do not share jobs from Spotlight or any other platform. Our agents manually send job details directly to talent.
Why does Next Stage Talent use the term "casting agents" rather than "talent agents"? +
Following feedback, we reviewed the language used across our website and updated it. We now use the term "talent agent" rather than "casting agent" to better reflect the work we do. There is no single regulated standard for every job title in the UK talent industry and we continue to update our language as we grow.
Does Next Stage Talent "place" talent or "secure auditions"? +
Both. We actively submit talent profiles for auditions and castings, and we work to connect our talent with real paid opportunities across film, TV, commercials, and modelling. What we control is the quality and consistency of our submissions. The final decision always rests with the casting director or client based on their specific brief.

How does Next Stage Talent help with casting jobs? +

With our industry network and experienced casting professionals, we streamline the process to find the right talent for each project, covering film, television, commercials, stage productions, fashion, and advertising. Our services include talent scouting, audition coordination, and contract negotiations.
What makes Next Stage Talent different from other agencies? +
We focus on being fully transparent from the start — about fees, how the industry works, and what is and isn't in our control. We believe in building long-term relationships with talent rather than chasing quick commission.
