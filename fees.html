What You Get — Free

When you are taken on by Next Stage Talent, the following are provided at no cost to you:

  • A dedicated agent who handles your submissions
  • A profile on Castly (normally £4.99/month — free for life while with us)
  • A digital Z-card to share with casting directors
  • Fortnightly job lists so you can see what we are actively submitting for
  • Self-tape guidance when you receive a casting brief
  • Submission via Spotlight and our direct industry contacts

None of these services cost you anything upfront. We cover these costs because we only make money when we secure work for you.

Commission FAQ — Quick Answers

Does Next Stage Talent charge a registration fee?

No. Applying to and joining Next Stage Talent is completely free.

Are there ongoing monthly or annual fees?

None whatsoever. No retainer fees, no subscription costs, no annual charges. The only payment we ever receive is our 20% commission on work we secure for you.

Is 20% commission normal for talent agencies?

Yes. A 20% commission rate is entirely standard across UK talent and casting agencies. Some charge more; we keep ours at the industry benchmark.

When exactly does Next Stage Talent take commission?

Only when we successfully place you in paid work. Commission is taken from your earnings at that point — you never pay anything upfront to us.

Any Other Questions?

We're happy to answer anything about our fees or process before you apply. No question is too small — we'd rather you fully understand how we work than have any doubts.
