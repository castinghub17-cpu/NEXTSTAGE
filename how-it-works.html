Complete Transparency

Understanding Our Approach

Next Stage Talent is a casting and talent representation agency. We submit talent for real paid roles in film, TV, theatre, commercials, and advertising. We use Spotlight and direct industry relationships to connect our talent with casting directors. We do not run workshops. We do not charge fees to join.

This page explains exactly how our process works, what applicants can expect, and how we find and submit for opportunities. Everything here is free for the talent we represent.

New or Developing Talent

Not every applicant follows the same pathway. Each application is assessed individually.

The Process

Step by Step — From Application to Booking

1

Submit Your Application

The process begins when you submit your application — completely free. This allows our team to assess your current experience, existing materials, presentation standards, industry readiness, and suitability for available opportunities.

2

Talent Assessment

Following your application, your profile is carefully reviewed. Applicants are generally placed into one of two pathways:

Pathway A — Industry Ready

Applicants who already meet professional presentation standards may be considered for direct opportunity submissions.

Pathway B — Developing Talent

Applicants who show potential but are not yet fully industry-ready may require further preparation before progressing.

3

Profile Check (If Needed)

If you do not yet have professional headshots, a Spotlight profile, or a Z-card, we advise on what to get in place before we begin submissions. This guidance is free. Next Stage Talent does not run workshops, charge for training, or require you to purchase anything from us.

Many applicants skip this step entirely because they already have suitable materials. Experienced actors and existing Spotlight members go straight to Step 4.
4

Profile Preparation

Once an applicant is ready, their professional materials are prepared for submissions — ensuring talent are presented professionally when opportunities arise.

5

Representation & Opportunity Access

Once assessed as industry-ready, suitable talent are assigned a dedicated talent representative who reviews casting opportunities, supports submission selections, and forwards professional materials to relevant casting professionals — including via Spotlight, the UK's leading casting platform.

6

Work Secured — 20% Commission

When we successfully place you in a paid role, we take our standard 20% commission. This is the only time Next Stage Talent earns anything. No booking = no commission.

Submissions

How Opportunity Submissions Work

1

Opportunities Become Available

Our team identifies suitable opportunities based on current casting requirements from production companies, brands, and casting directors.

2

Opportunities Are Shared

Suitable opportunities are shared with relevant talent on our books based on their profile, look, and availability.

3

Talent Choose

Talent select which opportunities they would like to be considered for.

4

Submission

Their professional materials are forwarded to the relevant casting professionals.

5

Casting Decisions

Casting professionals make all final shortlisting and selection decisions independently.

Important: Being submitted for opportunities does not guarantee shortlisting, auditions, selection, or paid work. Final decisions are always made independently by casting professionals.

Quick Answers

Representation — Common Questions

How long is the contract with Next Stage Talent?

We operate on an open contract with no fixed time frame. You remain with us for as long as it is working for both parties. There is no lock-in and no cancellation fee.

Can I be with other agencies at the same time?

Yes, absolutely. Being represented by multiple agencies simultaneously is completely permitted and very common in this industry. We encourage talent to maximise their exposure.

Is Next Stage Talent registered on Spotlight?

Yes. Our agents are registered on Spotlight, one of the most widely used casting platforms in the UK — used by casting directors across film, television, theatre, and commercials.

Do professional actors or Spotlight members need to do anything extra?

No. If you are already a professional actor or Spotlight member, you are considered industry-ready. We can represent you straight away — no additional steps required. We simply take our 20% commission on any work we secure for you.

What commission does Next Stage Talent take?

We take a standard 20% commission only when we successfully secure paid work for you. There are no upfront fees, no retainers, and no ongoing charges.
