What Next Stage Talent Actually Does

We are a casting and talent representation agency. We find work for actors, models, and commercial talent by submitting their profiles directly to casting directors, production companies, and brands.

We do not just put talent on a database and wait. We actively match each person's skills and type to live casting briefs and make targeted submissions on their behalf. When casting directors respond with feedback or an audition offer, we pass that on straight away.

Development at Next Stage Talent happens through real industry experience, not workshops or classroom sessions. Getting in front of casting directors, receiving professional feedback, and building credits over time is how careers in this industry actually grow.

Who We Represent

Trained and Experienced

Actors and Models With Existing Credentials

If you have professional training, drama school credits, Spotlight membership, previous agency experience, or a strong existing portfolio, we can begin representing you straight away. No portfolio investment required. We start submitting your details for relevant roles immediately.

New Talent

If you are new to acting or modelling, we first assess what material you have. If your existing photos and presentation meet industry standard, we can move quickly to submissions. Do We Guarantee Work?

No.

No legitimate agency can guarantee work. Casting decisions are made by casting directors and clients, not by us.

What we do guarantee is that we actively work on your behalf. We submit you for roles, track the feedback, and work with you to improve your chances over time. If you are not getting selected, we find out why and address it.

Addressing Questions Raised Online

We are aware that some online discussions have raised questions about certain aspects of how we operate. We want to address each one directly and honestly.

Why are client CVs not displayed publicly?

The majority of our talent are covered under NDA (non-disclosure agreement). This means we are contractually permitted to share their names, but not their full CVs or personal details publicly. This is a protective measure for our talent, not something to hide. Casting directors who enquire about a specific talent receive full details directly through us.

Did you previously display ages for under-18 talent?

Yes, we did previously list ages as a standard way to help casting teams identify suitable talent quickly. Following feedback from the industry and our talent families, we have since updated our practices and no longer display ages publicly. We took that feedback on board and acted on it.

Why were Spotlight job listings showing on Next Stage Talent pages?

Any Spotlight job listings that appeared on our pages were part of a test page that was never meant to go live. It was a technical error and those pages have since been removed. We do not share jobs from Spotlight or any other platform. Our agents manually send job details directly to talent.

What about the terminology used, such as "casting agents"?

Following feedback, we reviewed the language used across our website and updated it. We now use the term "talent agent" rather than "casting agent" to better reflect the work we do. There is no single regulated standard for every job title in the UK talent industry and we continue to update our language as we grow.

Does Next Stage Talent "place" talent or "secure auditions"?

We do both. We submit talent for castings and auditions, and we work to connect our talent with real paid opportunities across film, TV, commercials, and modelling. The end result depends on the casting director's requirements and the individual talent. What we control is the quality and consistency of our submissions on your behalf.

How to Know If Any Agency Is Legitimate

Signs of a legitimate talent agency:

  • No upfront registration or joining fee
  • Clear explanation of how commission works
  • Honest about the fact that work is not guaranteed
  • Transparent about any third-party costs such as photography
  • Does not pressure you to sign or pay quickly
  • Has a real address and contact details

Next Stage Talent meets all of these. If you have any questions before applying, contact us first. No question is too small.
