About Next Stage Talent — London

We are based in London — the heart of the UK's film, television, commercial, and fashion industries — which gives us direct access to some of the biggest casting opportunities in the country.

While our base is London, we work with talent from across the UK. Castings increasingly take place online or in flexible locations, and many of our opportunities are open to talent regardless of where they are based.

Next Stage Talent is a casting and representation agency. We submit talent for real paid roles. We do not run workshops, we do not sell training courses, and we do not charge to join. We only earn a 20% commission when we secure paid work for you.

We work nationally and internationally — so even if you are not in London, you can still apply and be considered for the full range of opportunities we handle.

What Next Stage Talent Does

Casting Services

We provide comprehensive casting solutions for film, television, commercials, stage productions, and advertising. Our network of industry professionals and casting contacts means we can connect the right talent to the right role efficiently.

Talent Representation

We represent models, actors, presenters, dancers, and commercial talent of all ages, sizes, and backgrounds. We submit talent via Spotlight and through our direct relationships with production companies and brands including BBC, Netflix, Nike, NHS, and more.

No Registration Fee

Joining Next Stage Talent costs nothing. We operate entirely on commission — 20% taken only when we successfully place you in a paid role. There are no upfront agency fees, no retainer, and no subscription.

Open Contract

We do not lock talent into long-term contracts. You are free to be registered with other agencies simultaneously, and there is no fixed term on representation. You stay with us for as long as it works for both parties.

Who We Work With

We represent talent across a wide range of categories:

  • Actors — film, TV, theatre, commercials
  • Models — fashion, editorial, commercial, plus size, mature
  • Extras and background artists
  • Presenters and hosts
  • Dancers and performers
  • New and developing talent

We welcome talent of all ages, ethnicities, and backgrounds. The industry has changed significantly in recent years, and diversity is a genuine requirement — not just a buzzword — for the vast majority of castings we handle.
