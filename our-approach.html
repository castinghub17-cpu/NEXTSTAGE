Who We Are

A Representation Agency That Develops Careers Through Real Work

Many people searching online find Next Stage Talent described as a "developing agency" , but that description misses the most important part of what we do. We are first and foremost a talent representation and casting agency.

We develop talent by placing them in real industry work and not by putting them through a training programme and leaving them to find their own opportunities. When you are on our books, our team is actively looking for castings, auditions, and jobs that suit your type and skillset, then submitting you directly to the decision-makers.

That is how careers in this industry are built: through real experience, real feedback from casting directors, and real credits that build a professional portfolio over time.

"The best way to develop as an actor or model is to be in the room. Our job is to get you there."

New Talent

No Experience Required

If you are new to the industry, we look at your natural type, energy, and presence and then find roles that suit you at the right level. Everyone starts somewhere.

Trained & Experienced

We Represent Trained Performers Too

If you have formal training, drama school credits, previous agency experience, or a professional portfolio, we factor all of that in when matching you to casting opportunities. Trained actors and experienced models are absolutely welcome.

Our Process

How We Find Work for Our Talent

Our casting and representation process is designed to give every person on our books the best possible chance of being seen by the right people. Here is exactly how it works:

1

Online Application

You apply online for free through our website. Our team reviews every application personally. Suitable applicants are invited to the next stage and that is a Zoom video meeting with one of our team.

2

Zoom Meeting: Getting to Know You

Your first meeting with us takes place over Zoom. This is where we get to know you : your natural presence, your personality, how you come across on camera, and what kind of roles you might be suited for. We are not just looking at your photos; we are assessing you as a whole person. If we believe you have the qualities we are looking for, we invite you to meet us in person.

3

In-Person Meeting: Going Deeper

At this stage we meet you face to face for a more detailed skills assessment. We spend time understanding exactly what you bring : your natural abilities, your look, your versatility, and the specific types of roles where you would genuinely shine. This deep understanding of your skills is what allows us to match you intelligently to casting briefs rather than simply firing your profile at everything.

4

Skills Matching: Finding the Right Jobs for You

Using everything we have learned about you, we match your natural skills and type against the live casting briefs and job opportunities we hold. We only submit you for roles we genuinely believe are right for you. This targeted approach means casting directors take our submissions seriously and they know we do not send names unless we mean it.

5a

Trained Actors: We Start Straight Away

If you have previous professional training (drama school, acting classes, previous agency representation, screen credits) we move quickly. Trained performers are onboarded and we begin actively sending their details for relevant jobs straight away. No waiting, no additional hoops. You are represented from day one.

5b

New Talent: Profile Review and Honest Advice

For talent who are newer to the industry, we first review the material you have : your photos, showreel footage, and CV. If your existing material meets industry standard, we can begin submissions quickly. If it needs updating, we tell you honestly what is required: whether that is new professional headshots, a showreel, or simply updated photos. We only begin actively submitting you once your profile is strong enough to represent you properly to the industry.

6

Agent Assignment & Regular Job Lists

Once your profile is complete and ready, you are assigned a dedicated agent at Next Stage Talent. Your agent actively monitors casting briefs and opportunities on your behalf, and sends you a curated list of relevant jobs and castings on a regular basis , usually every fortnight. You always know what is being done on your behalf.

7

Feedback & Ongoing Improvement

After every submission, we track the feedback from casting directors and clients. If you are not being selected, we find out why. We then work with you to improve the specific areas that need attention: sometimes that means updating your headshots, improving how you perform on camera, the roles you are being put forward for, or how your profile is presented.

One of the most common reasons new talent does not get accepted is the quality of their self-tape. A self-tape is often the first thing a casting director sees, and if it does not hit the right standard, the opportunity is gone before it even begins. We take this seriously. We provide example videos and guidance to help our talent understand exactly what a good self-tape looks like and how to record one properly at home. All of this support is completely free of charge. We are invested in your progress because your success is our success.

Continuous improvement driven by real industry feedback is at the heart of how we develop careers.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Representation

Is Next Stage Talent a casting agency or a development agency?

Both — but representation and casting submissions are at the core of what we do. We are a casting and talent representation agency. The development of your career happens through the real castings, real feedback, and real experience we provide. We are not a training school.

Do you send talent directly to casting directors?

Yes. We submit talent profiles directly to casting directors, production companies, brands, and photographers. Our submissions are tailored. We do not mass-blast your profile. We select roles we genuinely believe you are right for and present you professionally.

Can trained actors join Next Stage Talent?

Absolutely. We represent trained actors and experienced performers. If you have drama school training, previous agency representation, screen credits, or a professional showreel, our team will factor all of that into how we represent and submit you. Trained talent often accesses higher-level castings through our books.

What if I have no experience at all?

That is fine too. We assess your natural type and suitability, then find appropriate castings at the right level. Many of the most successful commercial faces and character types are people with no formal training with the right look, energy, and reliability. We help you build your career from the ground up.

What areas of the UK do you work in?

We are based in London but represent talent from across the United Kingdom. Many of the castings and opportunities we submit for are national (including remote voice-over work, UK-wide advertising campaigns, and productions based outside London). We regularly represent talent from Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Sheffield, Bristol, and beyond.

How do I know if I am being submitted for castings?

We keep our talent informed. When we submit your profile for a casting, we aim to let you know. If a casting director responds positively or requests an audition, we contact you directly with the details, brief, and any preparation required.
