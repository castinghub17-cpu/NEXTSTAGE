Common Questions About Next Stage Talent

These are the questions we see most often when people search for Next Stage Talent reviews online.

Is Next Stage Talent a legitimate agency?

Yes. Next Stage Talent is a legitimate UK-based casting and modelling agency. We operate transparently, charge no upfront registration fees, and take commission only when we secure paid work for you. See our dedicated legit page for a full explanation.

Are there any negative reviews of Next Stage Talent?

Like any agency, we have had the occasional misunderstanding — usually around portfolio costs or expectations about how quickly bookings happen. We believe in addressing concerns directly and being honest that the industry takes time. We encourage anyone with a concern to contact us first before posting online.

How does Next Stage Talent compare to other agencies?

Unlike some agencies, we do not charge joining fees. We operate on a commission-only model (20%) and allow our talent to be registered with multiple agencies simultaneously. We also submit talent via Spotlight and maintain direct relationships with casting directors and production companies.
