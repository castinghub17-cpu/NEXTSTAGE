'Additional Usage' means any further usage (in addition to the Permitted Use) granted to the Client to use the Artist's image or reproductions, or adaptations thereof for a purpose, territory and/or time limit to be negotiated and agreed between Next Stage Talent and the Client.

'Booking' means any services requested of the Artist by a Client in connection with any form of advertising, entertainment, leisure, marketing, public relations activities, acting, television or any other medium of work undertaken through Next Stage Talent in consideration of the Booking Fee.

'Client' means any third party individual, partnership, company or other organisation which contracts with Next Stage Talent with a view to procuring the services of the Artist.

'Client Supplement' means a supplement of 20% which will be added to the Booking Fee and invoiced to the Client.

'Commission' means the percentage of the Booking Fee retained by Next Stage Talent.

'Normal Rates' means the normal hourly, half day and full day rates charged by Next Stage Talent for each Booking. These rates are available on request.

'Permitted Use' means the agreed right granted to the Client to use the Artist's image or reproductions for the purpose, territory and time limit agreed and confirmed by Next Stage Talent on the Booking Form.

4. Booking Fees

4.1 Booking Fees are agreed on the information provided by the Client regarding Permitted Use and duration of Booking. Any Additional Usage must be negotiated with Next Stage Talent.

4.2 Booking Fees will be calculated by Next Stage Talent and agreed with the Client in advance of any Booking.

4.3 Overtime rates apply on all Bookings in excess of any 8 hour period. Overtime/special rates also apply to Bookings outside of the hours of 09:00 and 18:00:

  • One and a half times the Normal Rates between 18:00 and 24:00
  • Double the Normal Rates on Sundays and bank and public holidays
  • One and a half times the Normal Rates on Saturdays between 09:00 and 24:00
  • A special rate will be negotiated for night work between 24:00 and 09:00

Any time spent by the Artist travelling to or from a Client's venue will be charged at half the Normal Rate for Bookings outside the M25 region in London.

All expenses incurred by Next Stage Talent on the Client's behalf will be charged to the Client and will include an uplift of 12.5% of the total amount of the expenses.

5. Additional Usage Fees

5.1 Additional Usage fees must be negotiated and agreed with Next Stage Talent prior to the Client using the images or footage of the Artist for anything other than the Permitted Use.

5.2 The Client shall pay Additional Usage fees for any Additional Usage, to be negotiated and agreed with Next Stage Talent.

5.3 It is the Client's responsibility to notify Next Stage Talent of any proposed Additional Usage. In the event that Next Stage Talent is not notified and subsequently becomes aware of the Additional Usage, Next Stage Talent reserves the right to invoice the Client the amount deemed reasonable and appropriate.

6. Next Stage Talent Fee

6.1 All Booking Fees charged for the right to use images, once agreed, are payable by the Client irrespective of whether such images are subsequently used.

6.2 Next Stage Talent charges a Client Supplement on all Bookings (excluding equity contracts and equity contract TV commercials).

6.3 The Client Supplement will not apply in relation to BBC and Equity contracts and Equity Contract TV Commercials.

6.4 All Booking Fees relating to music videos will be negotiated between Next Stage Talent and the Client on a Booking by Booking basis.

6.5 All Booking Fees relating to fashion shows will confer the right to use photographs and video of the show for reporting purposes only. Any other usage must be disclosed and an Additional Usage fee negotiated and agreed with Next Stage Talent.

7. Provisional Bookings

Provisional bookings are permitted by Next Stage Talent to permit the Client to reserve the Artist. Provisional bookings will be automatically cancelled if they are not confirmed by the Client within 24 hours of the proposed booking.

8. Cancellations

8.1 Where a Booking is cancelled by the Client within a 24 hour period of the start time, the full Booking Fee will be charged unless the same Artist is rebooked within 24 hours of cancellation (in which case only half the booking fee will be charged).

8.2 Where a Booking is cancelled outside of the 24 hour limit but within 48 hours of the start time, then half the Booking Fee will be charged.

8.3 Where a Booking of more than three days' duration is cancelled within a period equal to the length of the Booking, the full Booking Fee will be charged.

8.4 Next Stage Talent shall use reasonable endeavours to provide the Client with reasonable notice of any cancellation by Next Stage Talent and shall offer a suitable Artist replacement.

8.5 Weather related cancellations: on the first occasion half the Booking Fee will be charged; on the second occasion (and thereafter) the full Booking Fee is charged.

9. Meals

Clients are responsible for the provision of all meals and beverage requirements of the Artist (taking into account dietary requirements) whilst the Artist is providing services to the Client.

10. Invoices

10.1 Both the Booking Fee and (where applicable) the Client Supplement will be invoiced by Next Stage Talent.

10.2 Unless otherwise agreed, the invoice will include the Artist disbursement at 66.6% and Next Stage Talent's disbursement at 33.3% of the invoice total (except in relation to Equity contracts, where it is 80%/20%).

10.3 Payment of invoices is required within 30 days of the date of the invoice.

10.4 All fees for the Permitted Use are for the right to use the Artist's image and, once agreed, are payable whether or not such right is exercised.

10.5 If the Client fails to pay on the due date, the amount outstanding shall bear interest at five per cent per annum over Barclays Bank plc base rate from time to time from the due date until payment is made in full.

11.2 An additional fee will be negotiated when the use of the Artist's image is required on an exclusive or semi-exclusive basis. It is the Client's responsibility to check whether the Artist supplied has undertaken any conflicting work.

12.3 All usage of any test or experimental photography must be fully disclosed to Next Stage Talent prior to such photography taking place.

The Client shall ensure that the Artist is treated with respect and professionalism. The Client warrants that it will ensure that the safety, health and well being of the Artist is protected and maintained at any Booking, including:
